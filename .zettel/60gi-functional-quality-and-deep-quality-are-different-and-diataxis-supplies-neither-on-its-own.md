Functional quality is accuracy, completeness, consistency, precision, usefulness. It is measured against the product. Its dimensions are independent: a page can be accurate and incomplete, or complete and useless. It constrains the writer.

Deep quality is how the documentation feels to use: flow, fit to human need, anticipation of the reader, beauty. It is assessed against experience, not measured. Its aspects reinforce each other, and it rests on functional quality being there first.

Diátaxis cannot create functional quality. It exposes lapses, because a structural inconsistency shows up as a page that will not classify. It does reach part of deep quality, by organising content around need and protecting the flow of each form. The site says plainly that it can never be all that good documentation requires.
