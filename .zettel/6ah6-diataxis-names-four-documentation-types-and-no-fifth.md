Diátaxis identifies four needs a reader brings to documentation, and one form of documentation for each: tutorial, how-to guide, reference, explanation. The claim is not that four is a convenient number. Two distinctions cross to make four quadrants, and the site states that the grid leaves nothing over.

There is simply no other territory to cover.
