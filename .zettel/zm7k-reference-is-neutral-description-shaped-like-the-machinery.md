Reference describes the machinery: commands, options, flags, classes, limits, warnings, error messages. It is consulted during work, never read through.

<!-- prov citation:diataxis-reference -->
A map tells you what you need to know about the territory, without having to go out and check the territory for yourself; a reference guide serves the same purpose for the product and its internal machinery.
<!-- /prov -->

Two rules carry the form. Structure mirrors the product, because a reader who knows the product then knows where to look. Description stays neutral: facts, not instruction, not opinion, not speculation. Consistent patterns across entries are what make reference usable at all. Examples illustrate; they do not explain.
