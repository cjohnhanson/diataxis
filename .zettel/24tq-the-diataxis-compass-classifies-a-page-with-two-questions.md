The compass asks two questions of a piece of documentation. Does it inform action or cognition? Does it serve acquisition of skill or application of skill? The pair of answers names the type.

Action and acquisition gives a tutorial. Action and application gives a how-to guide. Cognition and application gives reference. Cognition and acquisition gives explanation.

The compass is a correction tool, not a planning tool. The site aims it at the author who already thinks the page is one thing and doubts it. The two questions apply at any scale, from one sentence to a whole document.
