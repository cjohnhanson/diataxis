Sometimes the reader is at study, and sometimes the reader is at work. That single difference separates [[be9g]] from [[7g3y]].

The site takes its example from suturing. A tutorial is the medical school exercise: a controlled setting, a supervised path, a beginner acquiring basic competence. A how-to guide is the clinical manual: a competent practitioner, a real patient, and conditions the writer cannot predict.

Four differences follow. A tutorial manages the path; a guide prepares for the unexpected. A tutorial puts responsibility on the teacher; a guide puts it on the user. A tutorial avoids choices; a guide forks. A tutorial is concrete and particular; a guide has to generalise.

Conflating them in medicine is deadly. In software it turns away the newcomers the project wanted.
