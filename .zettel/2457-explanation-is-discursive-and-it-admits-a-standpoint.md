Explanation is understanding-oriented. It answers "can you tell me about...?". It is read away from the work, and the site calls it the one kind of documentation you might read in the bath.

It makes connections, including to things outside the product. It gives context: why a decision went the way it did, what constrained it, what the alternatives were. It admits opinion, because understanding has a point of view and pretending otherwise helps nobody.

Its boundary is a topic, not a task or a lesson. Other names for the same thing: discussion, background, conceptual guide, topics.
