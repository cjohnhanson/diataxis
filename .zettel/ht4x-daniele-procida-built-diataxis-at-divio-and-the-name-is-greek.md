Daniele Procida created Diátaxis. The work ran at Divio between 2014 and 2021, and the original scope was software product documentation. Procida is a Django core developer and a director of engineering at Canonical.

In 2021 a fellowship from the Software Sustainability Institute funded work on applying it outside software, to scientific research, to internal corporate documentation, to organisational management and education, and at scale. Readers have taken it further, to note-taking systems and to running a household.

The site glosses the name as Greek: dia, across, and taxis, arrangement. The word has an older sense that the framework did not invent. In the Eastern Orthodox church a diataxis is a rubric book that directs a priest through a service. Those appear in the tenth century, the earliest surviving copies are twelfth century, and the diataxis of Philotheos Kokkinos became the authoritative one during his patriarchate, 1364 to 1376. The Latin equivalents are ordo and directorium.
