Diátaxis prescribes no plan and no restructuring project. It works from the inside, in small responsive steps, and the overall structure emerges from them.

The loop has four moves. Choose something, anything, without hunting for the worst problem first. Assess it against reader need. Decide on one improvement. Do it, and publish it. Then repeat.

The instinct it fights is the batch: holding a large tranche of work back until it is finished. Every step in the right direction is worth publishing on its own. Documentation on this model is always complete at each stage and never finished.
